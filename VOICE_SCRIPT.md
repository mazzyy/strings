# Voice recording script: Marlo, and the cast's cheers for Sir Pip

Record these yourself and they replace the computer voice line by line. Any line you don't
record keeps its current voice, so you can do them in any order and send them in batches.

## How to record
- **Equipment:** a phone voice recorder is fine. Find a quiet room with soft furnishings (a bedroom or wardrobe beats a kitchen), hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, a little to one side so you don't pop the "p"s.
- **Format:** WAV or M4A (any is fine). Name each file exactly as in the table.
- **Takes:** leave half a second of silence before and after. Do 2 takes of the important ones (marked ★) and add `_b` to the second one (e.g. `marlo_task_shy_b.wav`); I'll pick the best.
- **Length:** the **Target** column is how long the line runs now. Anywhere within about ±30% is fine: natural beats exact.
- **Wording:** say it exactly as written (the subtitles show the same words). Small natural extras ("oh!", a laugh) are welcome.
- **Afterwards:** I trim, match loudness, and can raise the pitch slightly to make a dragon sound younger, so record in your natural voice.

## Who Marlo is
A young dragon, small for his age, who breathes only sparkles. Think of an excited puppy that is also a dragon: bright, quick, honest, easily delighted, a bit shy with strangers, and fiercely loyal once you're his friend. He is never sarcastic and never cross for long. Smile while you speak: it can be heard.
